    How the activity tracker works

    Model from the activity tracker project (project root)

    Every 0.5 s the last 2 seconds of readings are resampled to 40 samples at 20 Hz, converted to m/s², and turned into 28 statistics (mean, spread, range, RMS and median of X, Y, Z and overall strength). A Random Forest picks the activity; the display shows the most common of the last 3 predictions so it doesn't flicker. The micro:bit LEDs show it too.

    • The model was trained on the WISDM dataset: phones carried upright in a front trouser pocket by 36 people. Wear the micro:bit the same way for the best results.
    • It knows Walking, Jogging, Upstairs, Downstairs, Standing and Sitting. It was not trained on cycling.
    • The realistic accuracy for someone new is the "new people" figure below, not the test-set figure.
